How it started

From a balcony roaster to our own shop

Ember started with a home roasting machine on a balcony and three bean origins for friends. Five years on, we have our own roastery, regulars who know our names, and a line for fresh pastries on weekend mornings.

We still cup every new batch together as a team before it makes it onto the menu. If we don't love the beans, you won't find them here.
